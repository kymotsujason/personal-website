It's time for a professional redesign!

I've decided to start implementing a more professional looking design for the website. While it doesn't look too bad right now, it's definitely not anywhere near professional. I built the site with learning in mind, rather than professionalism. However, I'm planning to keep the old design and have already rerouted all the pages to v1.

Here's how the redesign will look: [Mockup/Wireframe](https://app.moqups.com/oSikYcTpom/view/page/ad64222d5?ref=blog.jasonyue.ca)

It's not colored or anything, but the implementation will follow a dark theme with alternating dark color grids that stretch to the ends of the screen, though content will be centered a little bit (eyes should see everything if you look at the middle of the screen). It's kinda similar to the card style design right now, but will be lined up on top of each other. I'm also planning to add a resume and contact page, since this is supposed to be my portfolio after all.

Hope to have the site redesign fully implemented before the next blog!
